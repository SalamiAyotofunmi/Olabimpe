Six Years of Us

Six years of friendship and a person I’ll always be grateful for.

If someone had told me back in secondary school that one day we would be miles apart, with you living in another country and us having to celebrate special moments through screens, I probably wouldn’t have imagined how much our friendship would still mean to me.

But here we are.

Six years later, and I can honestly say that having you as my friend is one of the things I’m genuinely grateful for in this life.

I don’t think I say it enough, but thank you. Thank you for being my friend, for being there for me, for supporting me when things aren’t easy, and for showing me that distance doesn’t have to mean disconnection.

You’ve been there for me emotionally, and you’ve helped me financially at times when things were tight. And honestly, those things mean more to me than I can fully explain. There have been moments when I needed someone in my corner, and you’ve shown up for me even when you weren’t physically here.

You didn’t have to do all that, but you did.

And I’ll never forget it.

Even the little things matter so much to me. You remembering my birthday, getting me things on Valentine’s Day, and finding ways to make me feel loved even from another country. Those moments remind me that I have someone who thinks about me, someone who remembers me, and someone who genuinely wants to see me happy.

It’s never just about what you give me. It’s about the heart behind it. It’s about knowing that somewhere in the world, even with your own life, responsibilities, and challenges, you still make space for me.

And I hope you know that I see it. I appreciate it. I appreciate YOU.

I hope life is kind to you. I hope this new chapter brings you closer to every dream you’ve ever had. I hope you experience the kind of love that makes you feel safe, the kind of happiness that doesn’t have to be forced, and the kind of success that makes you look back and realise that every sacrifice was worth it.

I hope you never feel alone in the pursuit of your dreams. I hope you always have people who show up for you the way you’ve shown up for me. And whenever life gets difficult, I hope you remember that you have someone back here who genuinely wants the best for you and will always be rooting for you.

I wish I could be there today to celebrate you in person, give you the biggest hug, laugh with you, and make more memories together. But until we get that chance, I hope these words remind you that you’re loved, valued, and appreciated, even from thousands of miles away.

Thank you for the six years. Thank you for the memories we’ve made and the ones we haven’t made yet. Thank you for being part of my life and for being someone I can look back on and say, I’m really glad I met her.

And if I could go back to secondary school and choose my people all over again, I’d still be happy that life brought me to you.

May this year be beautiful to you. May you receive more than you give, may you smile more than you cry, and may life surprise you with beautiful things.

I love you, and I will never take our friendship for granted.

Here’s to six years of us, and to all the years still waiting for us.

The Day You Left

I don’t think I’ll ever be able to explain how I felt when you left the country. It was such a bittersweet feeling because, on one hand, I was genuinely happy for you. I was happy that you were getting the opportunity to experience something new, grow, chase your dreams, and hopefully build the kind of life you wanted for yourself. You deserved that opportunity, and a part of me was so proud of you for taking that step.

But on the other hand, a part of me was sad because I knew things were going to be different. We had spent time together, and suddenly, the thought of not being able to see you whenever I wanted, gist with you in person, laugh over the most random things, or just have you around wasn’t the easiest thing to accept. It was one thing knowing you were leaving, but it was another thing realising that you were actually going to be so far away.

I was happy for you, but I was going to miss you so much. And I think that’s what made it so emotional for me. I wanted you to go out there and do amazing things, but selfishly, I also wished you could stay a little longer. I wished things could somehow remain the same, even though I knew you had to move forward with your life.

Looking back now, I’m proud of you. I’m proud that you’ve been out there, living your life, growing, and finding your way in a different country. And even though the distance has changed the way we get to experience our friendship, I’m grateful that it hasn’t taken away what we have.

If anything, your leaving taught me that some friendships are bigger than physical presence. Because even with all the miles between us, you’ve continued to show up for me.

That day, I was sad because I was losing the comfort of having you close, but I was happy because you were getting a chance to build something for yourself. I couldn’t have asked life to keep you here just because I would miss you. Sometimes loving someone also means being happy for them when their journey takes them somewhere you can’t follow just yet.

I miss the days when you were closer, and I wish we could make more memories together in person. But if I had to choose between having you close to me and seeing you get opportunities to become the person you want to be, I would still want you to have those opportunities.

Because your happiness matters to me, your growth matters to me, and you matter to me.
